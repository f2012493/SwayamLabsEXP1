# Prediction: zero-shot pick-and-place in simulation, task v2 (pre-registered 2026-10-15)

Written and committed **before any v2 attempt was run**. The git commit date is the
proof. Do not edit this file after the data exists; results go in
`studies/out/sim_pick_place_zero_shot_v2.md` and link here. The v1 prediction
(`predictions/2026-10-15-sim-pick-place-zero-shot.md`) and result
(`studies/out/sim_pick_place_zero_shot.md`) are unchanged.

## Why a v2

v1 scored 10/10 against a predicted 7/10. Its decision rule says that >= 9 successes
means the simulated task is too easy to be a baseline and the next version removes
the weld's 15 mm forgiveness and/or shrinks the funnel. The v1 audit added that the
weld "grasped" a wedged cube tilted 34 deg with the jaws 40 % open, and that every v1
release happened within 2.7 deg of yaw and 2.3 mm of the slot centre, so the 8 mm
funnel was never actually exercised.

## What changed (`SWAYAM_TASK_VERSION=2`; v1 remains the default and is unchanged)

1. **Grasp rule v2** (`swayam.arm.sim.SimArm.grasp_check_v2`, evaluated at the moment
   of a close command below 20 %, in the gripperframe site frame):
   - the jaws close from a gap wider than the 20 mm cube (the moving-jaw pad's lowest
     point, measured from the model's own mesh, is > 20 mm above the fixed-jaw face;
     that is any opening above ~12 %);
   - the cube body lies between the pads along the jaw axis: centre in
     [8 mm, gap - 8 mm] (2 mm of pad overlap allowed; the pads do not collide with it);
   - the cube centre is over the pads: within +-7 mm across them and between 45 mm
     behind and 6.3 mm ahead of the gripper site along the fingers;
   - the cube is within 10 deg of flat (one face normal within 10 deg of vertical).
   Otherwise `grasp_event` says "close: no grasp (...)" with the numbers. This is
   **still a simplified grasp**: no contact forces, no slip, no compliance.
2. **Funnel v2** (`sim/so101/scene_task_v2.xml`): outer funnel 4 mm per side instead
   of 8; lead-in still 1.5 mm; mouth half-width 16.5 mm at z 17.5. Reason for going
   under the CLAUDE.md "outer funnel >= 8 mm until Phase 0 measures the arm" default:
   that number is sized for 3.5 mm camera-guided placement error; the sim arm places
   within 0.5 mm, and the noisy condition below uses 2 mm RMS, so 4 mm keeps the
   funnel at >= 2x the error it is asked to absorb. It is a sim-only tightening; the
   printed tray for hardware stays at 8 mm until ARM ERROR is measured.
3. **Placement noise** (`SWAYAM_PLACE_NOISE_MM=2`, condition `v2-noise`): every
   `move_gripper_to` target gets an independent N(0, 1.41 mm) error per axis
   (2 mm RMS radial), seeded from the cube seed (`default_rng(10000 + seed)`),
   logged per move as `placement_noise_mm`, never returned to the subject. The
   returned `tcp` is the pose actually reached, as a wrist tag would show it.
4. The tool descriptions tell the subject the active rule and the noise, as v1's told
   it the 15 mm rule. The instruction, model, seeds, turn/time/budget caps, hook and
   scoring are identical to v1.

Scene characterisation done before this prediction (`studies/out/funnel_capture.md`,
no arm, no LLM): a flat cube dropped from 1 mm above the v2 mouth seats at offsets
<= 2 mm for yaw <= 20 deg and at 0 offset for yaw <= 15 deg; from 4 mm offset onward
about half the cells end jammed in the funnel at 33-47 deg of tilt. v1 jams too, but
from larger offsets. A jammed, tilted cube cannot be re-grasped under rule v2.

## Protocol (fixed before the run)

- Condition `v2` first: 10 attempts, seeds 0-9 in order, none dropped. Then condition
  `v2-noise`: 10 attempts, seeds 0-9. Infrastructure failures count as failures and
  are flagged. Run tags `zero-shot-v2-<stamp>-NN` and `zero-shot-v2n-<stamp>-NN`.
- Otherwise exactly the v1 protocol: fresh `claude -p` per attempt, model
  `claude-fable-5-1`, empty directory, only the 10 `swayam-arm` tools, safety hook
  on, max 60 turns / 15 min / $10 per attempt, scoring recomputed from `runs/` by
  `swayam.experiments.zero_shot` (success = verifier true after the last tool call).
- Feasibility: `tests/test_task_v2.py::test_v2_scripted_pick_and_place_passes_the_verifier`
  solves v2 in the same 9 commands as v1 (release with the cube bottom ~10 mm above
  the mouth, yaw corrected first).
- Planned spend: <= $25 for both conditions (v1 cost $7.65).

## Predictions

| Quantity | v2 | v2-noise |
|---|---|---|
| Successes over 10 attempts | **7 / 10** (Wilson 95%: 40-89%) | **5 / 10** (Wilson 95%: 24-76%) |
| Median MCP tool calls per attempt | **20** | **24** |
| Attempts with at least one refused grasp (`close: no grasp`) | **>= 3** | **>= 4** |
| Most likely failure mode | **Cube jammed tilted (> 10 deg) in the funnel or pocket, centre within 12 mm of the slot centre, after a release; every re-grasp then refused by the tilt rule.** Mechanism: in v1 the subject released with the cube bottom 4-10 mm below the funnel mouth, i.e. pressing the held cube into the funnel; with a 4 mm funnel that puts the cube bottom inside the pocket, so any xy error > 1 mm or yaw > ~3 deg presses it onto the rim, the soft weld lets it tilt, and it is released tilted. | same |
| Second failure mode | Cube resting flat on the tray outside the mouth (> 12 mm from the slot centre) after a release with the TCP mis-aimed; flat, so re-graspable, but the attempt runs out of turns or the subject stops early. | same, more often |
| Attempts that keep commanding motion after the verifier first passes | at most 1 | at most 1 |
| Irrecoverable events (cube off the table) | 0 | 0 |
| Attempts that end without ever calling the verifier | 0 | 0 |

Reasoning: v2 removes the recovery path that saved v1 attempt 2 (re-grasp of a tilted
cube) and narrows the one that saved attempt 1 (flat cube on the rim: still allowed
if re-grasped from a wide opening). The nominal plan (open 100 %, TCP to the cube
centre, close, lift, correct yaw, descend, release) still passes the v2 rule with
margin: the TCP offset puts the cube 11 mm from the fixed jaw inside the [8, 90] mm
window and centred on the pads. So the grasp rule mostly bites on re-grasps, and the
funnel on releases. The 2 mm noise is inside the grasp windows (5 sigma across the
pads, 2 sigma to the fixed jaw) but makes press-in releases miss the pocket more
often; I expect it to cost about two attempts.

## Decision rule

- **v2 success-rate prediction holds** if measured successes are in **[5, 9]** of 10;
  **v2-noise** holds if in **[3, 7]**.
- **Tool-call predictions hold** if each median is in **[12, 35]**.
- **Failure-mode prediction holds** if, among failed attempts of a condition, the
  modal final state is "cube tilt > 10 deg and centre within 12 mm of the slot
  centre" (computed from the logged final pose), or there are no failures.
- If v2 >= 9 again: the sim is still too easy. v2-noise becomes the sim reference for
  the hardware rerun, and there is **no further sim hardening** before the arm
  arrives (the remaining forgiveness is the weld itself; only hardware removes it).
- If v2 <= 4: the first suspect is the tooling. Before changing anything, read every
  `close: no grasp` and `refused` record; if more than half the failures involve a
  grasp refused while the logged cube position was inside the pad windows, the next
  prediction is about a tooling fix, not the model.
- If v2 minus v2-noise >= 3 successes: the "funnel >= 2x placement error" design rule
  did not hold for this geometry in sim; it must be re-measured on the real tray
  before any fixture is sized by it.
- Whatever happens, the same instruction and seeds are rerun on hardware in Phase 0
  step 4, with the v2 (and v2-noise) numbers as the sim reference.
